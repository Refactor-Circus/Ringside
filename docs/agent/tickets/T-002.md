## Problem

Factory Prepare runs `vendor/bin/ringside` "From job.root", which depends on the working directory and escalated at prepare repeatedly. A dirty main, a missing remote and an inapplicable `ringside check` only surface after the build.

## Desired outcome

- Every `ringside` call in `resources/workflows/ring-master-factory.js` runs through `composer --working-dir=job.root exec ringside --`.
- Prompts say never to `cd` into the worktree.
- The Prepare preflight returns `dirty`, `remote` and `check_applies`. The script stops `needs-decision` at prepare (dirty, or no remote with a PR to open), or drops `ringside check` from the gate when `check_applies` is false.
- `factory.md` and the PREPARED schema mirror it.
- Workflow simulations and `vendor/bin/pest` pass.

## Out of scope

- Merging.
- Other packages.
- Changing RingMaster.
